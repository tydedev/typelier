## Build a stronger foundation for your story

Every novel starts with an idea.

But turning that idea into a complete story requires more than inspiration. Characters, themes, settings, and plot elements need space to develop and connect.

The **Novel Development Sheet** is a printable worksheet designed to help writers organize their thoughts and explore the foundations of their story before writing the first chapter.

---

## Develop your story with intention

A clear vision helps you make better creative decisions.

Use this sheet to explore the essential elements of your novel:

- the central idea behind your story
- the themes and emotions you want to communicate
- the characters that will guide the reader
- the world where your story takes place
- the direction of your plot

The goal is not to plan every single detail.

The goal is to understand the story you want to tell.

---

## What's included?

The worksheet includes dedicated sections for:

### Story Concept

Define the core idea of your novel and the elements that make it unique.

### Genre & Themes

Identify the genre, atmosphere, and main themes that shape your story.

### Characters

Explore your protagonist and important characters, including their motivations and conflicts.

### Setting & Worldbuilding

Collect ideas about the world, locations, and details that create the atmosphere of your novel.

### Plot Development

Organize your main story ideas and important narrative moments.

### Reader Experience

Consider the emotions and impressions you want your readers to experience.
