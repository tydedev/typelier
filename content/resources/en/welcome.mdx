## Your Typography Library

Choosing the right fonts for a book can take time. You need to consider the genre, the tone of the story, readability, and how different typefaces work together.

I created **Typelier** to make this process easier: a library of typography systems designed for books and long-form texts, where you can find inspiration and discover new font pairings.

## How Typelier Works

In the [Library](../library), you can explore typography systems using a variety of filters to find the ones that best fit your project. You can filter results by **genre**, **style**, **mood**, and **font category**, or search directly for a font by name.

**Every pairing in Typelier is personally curated and tested by me using fonts available through Google Fonts**. I select each combination based on factors such as readability, hierarchy, genre, and overall tone.

## Where to Start

If you're not sure which fonts to choose, start with the [Library](../library) and explore the different filters.

If you already have a font in mind, search for it directly to discover which pairings you can build around it.

Typelier is designed to help you move from *“Which font should I use?”* to making more informed typographic choices.
